Amazon Reviews: Data Cleaning & EDA

Investigated review-rating patterns and product engagement after preparing an Amazon product-review dataset for analysis.

  • Checked completeness and duplicate records.
  • Converted rating types and UNIX timestamps for analysis.
  • Visualized rating distributions and heavily reviewed products.
PythonPandasJupyterMatplotlib
Read project case study +

Question: What can product ratings and review counts tell us about customer engagement?

Method: Performed data-quality checks, corrected field types, and compared review distributions and product popularity.

Finding: Higher ratings represented a large share of reviews, while activity concentrated among a subset of products. This project explores ratings and counts; text-based sentiment modeling would be a future extension.
